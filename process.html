Training Documents

Grouped by training session (name & date) — browse by division to see everyone attending together, listed with each staff member's fee, or generate a cost breakdown letter for HRDF covering every company on the same session. Requisition Form downloads use the official Training Requisition Form template, one file per staff member (name, designation, division, date joined, training details, and fee are filled in automatically). Sections that require manual input — NRIC, HRDF Claimable, Training Bond, Justification, Recommendation, Expectation, and Approval — are left blank on the form for completion by hand or signature.
